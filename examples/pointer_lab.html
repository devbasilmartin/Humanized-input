Four more pointing tests

These cover what the ring test can't: odd shapes, moving targets, reacting to something appearing, and holding still. About four minutes in total.

  1. Shapes: click the teal square, then click the orange shape wherever feels natural.
  2. Moving: click the teal square, then click the orange box as it slides past.
  3. Reaction: rest the pointer on the teal square without clicking. Click the orange square as soon as it appears.
  4. Hold still: rest the pointer in the circle for three seconds without clicking.

Use the same mouse as last time, at your normal pace. If you miss, carry on.
