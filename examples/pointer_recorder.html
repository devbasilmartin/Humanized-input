Record how you point and click

Nine short blocks, about three minutes in total. Each block shows a ring of squares. Click the highlighted one, then the next one that lights up, across the ring.

  1. Work at your normal pace: quick, but don't rush.
  2. Use the device you normally use. A phone or touchscreen won't work, because it has no pointer path.
  3. If you miss, carry on. Misses are part of the data.
